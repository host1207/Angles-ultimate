Verb Number

Singular Verb (S.V)

Contains 's' at the end (is, was, has, does, plays) — except am!

Plural Verb (P.V)

Contains NO 's' at the end (are, were, have, do, play).

Opposite Law

Noun + s = Plural | Verb + s = Singular
