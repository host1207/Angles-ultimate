3. High-Yield Slot Traps: Preposition Wall, Let, & Gerunds

⚡ The 4 Deterministic Slot Checks (Click hidden answers to test yourself):
  • 1. Preposition Wall [P + PO]: Between you and , this is easy. / Everyone attended except .
  • 2. After "Let": Let you and finish this module.
  • 3. After "Than / As" (Match Same Slot): He runs faster than (run).
  • 4. Before a Gerund (V+ing acting as Noun): Use a Possessive Adjective! I appreciate helping me.
