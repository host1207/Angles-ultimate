5. Udaya Case (Possessive Adj) vs. Udayathu Case (Possessive Pronoun) & Apostrophes

📘 Key Distinctions & Orthography Checks:
  • Formula Lock: Udayathu Case (mine/ours/yours/his/hers/theirs) = Udaya Case (my/our/your/his/her/their) + Noun. (Note: his works for both slots!).
  • Her vs. Hers Slot Check:
    • Her car is big. (Followed immediately by noun car).
    • He spoke to her. (No noun after her; sits in object/PO space).
    • Hers is the perfect case. (Stands alone as Subject; equals Her case).
  • It's vs. Its:
    • It's = Contraction of It is / It has → a great day.
    • Its = அதனுடைய (Adhanudaya) Possessive Adjective → The camel has water in hump.
  • Singular vs. Plural Apostrophe:
    • Singular Noun: student's (ஒரு மாணவனுடைய).
    • Plural Noun ending in -s: students' (மாணவர்களுடைய — apostrophe only after s).
Mine understanding of the subject (A) / deepened further after the elaborate (B) / lecture delivered by the professor. (C) / No error (D)> Her is the perfect case (A) / that should be taken up (B) / on priority for consideration. (C) / No error (D)> my.>
