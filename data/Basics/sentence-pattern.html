Chapter 0: Sentence Pattern (SVOCA) & Verb Matrix

The Foundational X-Ray of English Grammar — Action vs. State Verbs, SVOCA Anatomy & Pronoun-Verb Mapping

1. Step 1: Always Find the Verb First (Action vs. State)

📘 Core Debugging Rule: Before looking for a Subject or Object, locate the Verb (V) and check its type:
  • Type 1 — Action Verb: Physical or mental action (e.g., eat, buy, give, paint, elect). The Subject is the doer of the action, and an Object (O) is possible.
  • Type 2 — State Verb (Be / Linking Verb): Expresses a state of being, identity, or condition (is, am, are, was, were, be, being, been, become, seem, turn). There is NO action, so an Object is IMPOSSIBLE. Instead, it acts like an equals sign (=) linking the Subject to a Complement (C).
💡 Tamil Logic & The "-ing" Subject Trap (Gerund):
  • State Verb Check: "He is a doctor" → Avan doctor-aga irukiran. Here, irukiran (is) shows a state, not an action. Since He = a doctor, a doctor is a Complement (C) → S + V + C.
  • Action Word as Subject: "Swimming is my passion" → Swimming ennudaya passion-aga irukirathu. Nobody is actively swimming in the water right now! Here, is is the state verb, Swimming acts as a Noun (Subject - S), and my passion represents Swimming (Swimming = my passion) → S + V + C.

3. Pronoun Person & Base Verb Matrix (Be / Have / Do / Action)

📘 Noun vs. Verb "-s" Flip Rule:
  • In Nouns, adding -s/-es makes it Plural (Boy → Boys).
  • In Action Verbs, adding -s/-es makes it Singular!
    • Plays = Singular Verb (S)
    • Play = Plural Verb (P)
  • The "I" & "You" Exceptions:
    • I (1st Person Singular): Takes am and was (Singular) for Be verbs, but takes Plural forms (have, do, play) everywhere else!
    • You (2nd Person Singular & Plural): Whether referring to 1 person or 100 people, You ALWAYS takes Plural verbs (are, were, have, do, play).
