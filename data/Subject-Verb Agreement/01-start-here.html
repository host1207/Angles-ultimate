Subject-Verb Agreement

Part 1: Foundational Agreement, Conjunctions & Subject Modifiers

Core Rule: Singular & Plural Subjects

📘 Golden Rule 1: Subject (Singular) → Verb (Singular)
a letter.> an apple.> sleeping in a bed.> drinking a beer.>
📘 Golden Rule 2: Subject (Plural) → Verb (Plural)
dancing in the hall.> playing in the field.>
to participate in the competition.> his homework on time.> necessary to maintain hygiene.> not coming today.> a doctor.>

Concept 1: Two Subjects Joined by "And"

📘 Golden Rule: When two or more distinct subjects are joined by 'and', a Plural Verb follows.
coming.> friends.> Hockey.> in shortage.> sold in this grocery shop.>

Concept 2: The Single-Idea Exception (Paired Nouns)

📘 Golden Rule: If two nouns joined by 'and' refer to a single person, thing, dish, or unified idea, a Singular Verb must be used.
my daily breakfast.> the race.> my favorite dish.>
⚠️ SSC Exam Trap: Check the context! "Bread and butter are sold here" (separate store items = plural) vs. "Bread and butter is my breakfast" (one combined dish = singular).

Concept 3: Articles & Adjectives with "And" (The Double-Determiner Rule)

📘 Golden Rule: Check the determiners: Articles (A, An, The), Demonstrative Adjectives (This, That, These, Those), and Possessive Adjectives (My, His, Her, Your, Our, Their, Its):
  • Determiner before the 1st noun ONLY → Refers to the same single person/thing → Singular Verb.
  • Determiner before BOTH nouns → Refers to two separate people/things → Plural Verb.
The Doctor and teacher arrived.> The Doctor and the teacher arrived.> The teacher and the students present.> A green and white shirt bought.> A green and a white shirt bought.> The honest and intelligent girl here.>
The Secretary and the Principal of the college attending the District Development Council Meeting.> The Principal and staff waiting for the chief guest.> The artist and writer died.> A hot and a cold spring found near each other.> The trustee and principal at loggerheads.> My friend, philosopher and guide come.>

Concept 4: Prepositional Connectors (Follow 1st Subject)

📘 Golden Rule: When two subjects are joined by the following connectors, the verb strictly agrees with the 1st Subject. Ignore the second subject!
The Prime Minister along with his Cabinet colleagues been welcomed by the Chief Minister.> He as well as you to go.> The captain along with his team practising very hard for the forthcoming match.>

Concept 5: Correlative Conjunctions (Nearest Subject Rule)

📘 Golden Rule: When two subjects are joined by these conjunctions, the verb strictly follows the Nearest Subject (the subject closest to the verb).
Jyoti done the crime.> her parents present to receive the award.> E-mail a method of exchanging digital messages.> the hall to be changed.> Rohit there at the shop when I went there.> Nikhil going to win the prize in the handwriting competition.>

Concept 6: "The + Adjective" = Plural Common Noun

📘 Golden Rule: The + Adjective of quality represents the entire class of people (e.g., The poor = poor people) and strictly takes a Plural Verb.
⚠️ SSC Exam Trap: Never attach an 's' to the adjective (never write "The poors" or "The blinds"). Keep The + base adjective and pair it with a plural verb!
The brave rewarded.> The rich the poor.> the brave but once.> The Poor always hard.> The Blind everyday.>
