📘 Deep-Dive Breakdown (Notebook + Exam Essentials):
  • 1. Affirmative vs. Assertive Nuance: In broad grammar, an Assertive (Declarative) Sentence (செய்தி வாக்கியம்) has two branches:
    • Positive (Affirmative): He fine. (Affirms a state or action).
    • Negative: He fine. (Negates a state or action using not, never, no, neither, seldom, hardly).
  • 2. Interrogative Inversion Lock: In a direct question ending with ?, the Helping/Auxiliary Verb MUST come BEFORE the Subject:
    • Direct Question: Where going? (Never ).
  • 3. Imperative Hidden Subject Proof (Question Tag Test): Because the hidden subject of every Imperative sentence is "You", its Question Tag always uses will you? or won't you? (and for Let us / Let's, the hidden subject is We → shall we?).
    • Shut the window, | Let's go for a walk,
  • 4. Exclamatory "What" vs. "How" Formula:
    • Use What (+ a/an) when a Noun is central: beautiful painting (Noun) it is!
    • Use How when only an Adjective or Adverb follows immediately: beautiful (Adj) the painting is!
