No Object

State verbs cannot have objects (takes SC instead).

Modal Verbs

Role Check

H.V: Yes  |  M.V: NO

Modal Lock

• No Agreement: Modals never add "-s".
• Modal + Base V1: Always followed by raw root V1 (be, have, do, play).

(55,29),(78,29),(78,4),(82,4),(82,20),(82,4),(92,4),(92,22)

Singular Verb

Identifier

has "s" mostly
(is, was, has, does, plays)

Exception

am (singular, but no "s")

Plural Verb

Identifier

has no "s" mostly
(are, were, have, do, play)

Concord

Base V1 without suffixes acts as plural.

Plural Scope

Singular/Plural distinction only exists in:
• Present Tense (V1) (plays vs play)
• Be Verbs (is/was vs are/were)
Past (V2) & Modals are blind to number.

(49,30),(26,30),(26,34),(8,34),(8,53),(8,34),(20,34),(20,53)

Main Verb

Conditions

1. Only one verb: Always Main Verb.
2. Two verbs present: 2nd verb is Main Verb.

Meaning Retained

• "do" verbs: Action meaning
• "have" verbs: Possession meaning
• "be" verbs: State meaning

Helping Verb

Condition

If two verbs present: The 1st verb is the Helping Verb (assists tense/aspect).

Meaning Void

When acting as Helping Verbs, "be", "have", and "do" carry no literal meaning.

 (52,31),(52,34),(35,34),(35,53),(35,34),(47,34),(47,53)

Finite Forms

Forms & Tense

• V1 (Base/Present): Direct present time.
• V2 (Past): Direct past time.
Carries tense independently.

Standalone Rule

Needs no helping verb to complete a sentence clause (Subject + V1/V2 = Valid sentence).

Non-Finite Forms

Forms & Aspect

• V3 (Past Participle): Perfect aspect.
• V4 (V1+ing / Pres. Participle): Continuous aspect.
Cannot convey tense alone.

The Auxiliary Lock

• V3 requires have/has/had or be.
• V4 requires a be verb.
Without an H.V., they act as Adjectives or Gerunds, never main verbs.
